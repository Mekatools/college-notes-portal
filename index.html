Privacy Policy

Student Data & Confidentiality Standards

1. Zero Student Tracking

EduVault does not track student activity, collect personal identifying information, or deploy advertising cookies. Browsing and downloading lecture notes is completely anonymous.

2. Cloud File Storage

All lecture notes, slide presentations, and syllabus documents are hosted directly on faculty professors' secure Google Drives and delivered over encrypted HTTPS connections.

3. Security Infrastructure

Faculty authentication and database records are governed by Cloudflare Edge Security with zero local machine storage.
